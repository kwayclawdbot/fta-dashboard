Options 101

The Right, Not the Obligation

Two contracts, two directions

A call is the right to buy. A put is the right to sell. That single choice — which direction you're betting on — decides everything else.

Call · bullish

Right to buy

Lock in the right to buy the underlying at the strike. You buy calls when you expect the price to rise.

Put · bearish

Right to sell

Lock in the right to sell the underlying at the strike. You buy puts when you expect the price to fall.

Both are derivative contracts: their value comes from something else — the stock or asset sitting underneath them.

In or Out of the Money

Is it worth something yet?

Moneyness answers one question: does the option hold real value right now, or is it all time and hope?

Call ITM

Price above the strike

A call is in-the-money when the price sits above the strike — you'd rather buy low while the market trades higher.

Put ITM

Price below the strike

A put is in-the-money when the price sits below the strike — you'd rather sell high than at the lower market.

Lock It In

Quick check

Five questions. Answer them to earn your points for the lesson.

1. What is an option?

An option is a derivative contract: it gives you the right, but never the obligation, to buy or sell the underlying at a set price.

2. Why would you buy a put option?

A put is the right to sell, so it gains value as the underlying falls. Buyers use puts for a bearish view or to hedge a decline.

3. Which three parts define every option contract?

Every contract has a strike (the agreed price), an expiration (the deadline), and a premium (what you pay). Delta, theta and vega are the Greeks — how it reacts, not what it is.

4. When is a call option in-the-money?

A call is in-the-money when the price sits above the strike — it carries real intrinsic value because you'd rather buy low while the market trades higher.

5. When you BUY an option, what is the most you can lose?

A bought option has defined risk: if it expires worthless, the premium you paid is your maximum loss — nothing beyond it.
