There are two classic ways to judge a stock, and they look at completely different things. Say you're deciding on a company. One way: read the company. Check its sales, its profits, its plans, like reading its report card. That's called fundamental analysis. The other way: read the price. Ignore the press releases and study what buyers and sellers are actually doing, right now, on the chart. That's technical analysis. One reads the company. The other reads the crowd.
