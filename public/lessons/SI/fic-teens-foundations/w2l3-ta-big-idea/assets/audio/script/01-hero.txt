Last lesson you learned the only rule of price: it's a tug of war, and the stronger side drags it their way. And you might have had a sneaky thought. If price is a fight, and the chart records that fight, couldn't you read the chart to see who's winning? Congratulations. You just invented technical analysis. Today you meet the big idea that powers everything chart traders do, including the next six lessons of this course. By the end, you'll know exactly what technical analysis is, what it claims, and, just as important, what it doesn't.
