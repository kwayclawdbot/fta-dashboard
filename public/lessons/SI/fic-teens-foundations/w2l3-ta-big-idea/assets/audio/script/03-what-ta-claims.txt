Here's the core belief of technical analysis, in one line: market behavior tends to repeat. Fear looks like fear on every chart. Greed looks like greed. So the patterns price carved out last month can hint at what it might do next. Notice the word: hint. Technical analysis is not a crystal ball, and anyone who sells it as one is lying to you. It deals in probabilities, not promises. A pattern says this usually leans that way. It never says this must happen. Traders use it to stack the odds, not to see the future.
