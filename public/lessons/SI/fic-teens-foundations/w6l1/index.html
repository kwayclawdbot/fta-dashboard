Set It Up · Place an Order

One minute to set up, then place an order

Set a starting balance, then treat that pretend money exactly like it's real: same sizing, same rules, same stop. Then you place practice orders. Every order ticket asks for the same handful of things.

⚡
Market order
Speed

Fills right now at whatever the current price is. Fast and certain to fill, but the exact price can shift a little as it goes through.

🎯
Limit order
Price

Fills only at your chosen price or better. You control the price you pay, but it only happens if the market actually reaches your number.

Lock It In

Quick check

Five questions. Show me the setup stuck and earn your points.

1. Your friend says they've been trading all week in an app, but never added any real money. What are they actually doing?

Paper trading rehearses real decisions with pretend money, so you can learn and make mistakes for free before risking real cash.

2. You open a paper account with a pretend balance. What's the smartest way to use it?

Treating pretend money like real money is the whole point. Cheating on a simulator just teaches you habits that lose real cash later.

3. The market's moving fast and you want in right now, at whatever the current price is. Which order type fits?

A market order fills immediately at the current price. It's fast, though the exact fill price can shift a little.

4. You'll only buy at your target price or better, and you're fine waiting. Which order gives you that control?

A limit order lets you control the price, but the trade only happens if the market actually reaches your number.

5. You're filling out a practice order ticket. Which set of details does it ask for?

Every order ticket asks for the symbol, buy or sell, the quantity, the order type, and often a stop.
