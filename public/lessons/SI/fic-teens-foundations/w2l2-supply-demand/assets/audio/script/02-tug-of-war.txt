Picture a literal tug of war rope. On one end: everyone who wants to buy the stock. That's demand. On the other end: everyone who wants to sell. That's supply. Say a sneaker drops in a limited run of one hundred pairs, and ten thousand people want one. What happens to the resale price? It rockets, because buyers are fighting each other for a scarce thing. Stocks work exactly the same way. When more people want to buy a stock than sell it, buyers have to offer more to get filled, and the price climbs.
