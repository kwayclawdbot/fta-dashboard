Last lesson you watched the machine at work: the stock market matched your buy order with someone else's sell order in under a second. But we left one question wide open. When that match happened, who set the price? Nobody at the exchange picks it. No boss types it in. The price comes from a tug of war, and today you learn to read it. This one idea, supply and demand, explains every price move you will ever see: every rally, every crash, every boring sideways day. Get this, and charts stop looking random. By the end, you'll be able to look at any imbalance between buyers and sellers and call which way the price goes next.
