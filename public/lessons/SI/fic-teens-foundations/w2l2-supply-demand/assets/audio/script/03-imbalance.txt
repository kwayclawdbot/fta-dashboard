Now flip it. Bad news hits a company, and suddenly everyone holding the stock wants out. Sellers flood the market, but buyers hang back. To find a buyer, each seller has to accept a little less, and a little less again, and the price stair steps down. So here's the rule, and it's the only rule. More buyers than sellers, price goes up. More sellers than buyers, price goes down. That's it. Not magic, not luck. An imbalance.
