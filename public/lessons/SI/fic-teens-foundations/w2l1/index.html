How hard did they win?

Lock It In

Quick check

Five questions. Show me you can read the scoreboard.

1. On a candlestick chart, a GREEN candle usually means:

Green means the close landed above the open, so the price rose and the buyers won the battle.

2. On a candle, what do the thin lines (wicks) show?

Wicks reach up to the high and down to the low, showing how far the fight swung before it settled at the close.

3. A long candle body tells you that period had:

A long body means the open and close are far apart, so one side had real force. A short body means it was a close fight, almost a tie.

4. A candle with a tiny body and a long lower wick is telling you:

The long lower wick shows how far price was shoved down before it snapped back. A tiny body means it nearly ended where it started — a stalemate with a warning.

5. Why do traders check more than one timeframe?

The same day looks choppy zoomed in and calm zoomed out. Checking both keeps a single candle in context instead of overreacting to noise.
