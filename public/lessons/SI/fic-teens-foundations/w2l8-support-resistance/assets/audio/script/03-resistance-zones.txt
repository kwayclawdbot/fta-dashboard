Now flip the picture upside down. The same stock rallies to around fifty and stalls there every single time. That ceiling is resistance: a price where sellers keep showing up, taking profits, cashing out. And one pro level detail before you mark your first level. Support and resistance are zones, not laser lines. Price bounced at forty, then thirty nine eighty, then forty ten? That's one fat floor around forty, not three different levels. Think of a painted stripe, not a tightrope. And the more times a zone gets tested and holds, the more traders trust it, and the harder they defend it.
