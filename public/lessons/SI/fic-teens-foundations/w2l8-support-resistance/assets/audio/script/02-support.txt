Start with the floor. Say a stock keeps sliding to right around forty dollars, and every time it gets there, it stops falling and bounces. Once, twice, three times. That level is support: a price where buyers keep showing up in force. Why there? Because thousands of traders remember it. Some missed the last bounce and are waiting to buy the dip. Others bought there before and will defend it. All that waiting demand acts like a safety net under the price.
