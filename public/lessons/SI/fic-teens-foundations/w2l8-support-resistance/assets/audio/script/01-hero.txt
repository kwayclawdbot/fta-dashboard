Last lesson you read the trend: staircases up, staircases down, and flat hallways in between. And a mystery came with it. In that flat hallway, price kept bouncing off the same floor and bonking into the same ceiling, over and over. Today you find out why. Those levels are called support and resistance, and they are the closest thing charts have to a map. Learn to spot them, and you'll suddenly know where the important fights happen before they start. By the end, you'll be able to mark the floor and the ceiling on any chart you see.
