The Floor and the Ceiling

Last lesson's mystery: in the flat hallway, price kept bouncing off the same floor and bonking the same ceiling. Those levels have names, support and resistance, and they're the closest thing charts have to a map.

The Floor

Support: where buyers keep showing up

The stock slides to ~$40 and bounces. Once, twice, three times. Why there? Memory. Some traders missed the last bounce and wait to buy the dip; others bought there and defend it. All that waiting demand is a safety net.

Three tests of the same floor · illustrative
The Ceiling · and a Pro Detail

Resistance: and why levels are zones

Flip the picture: rallies to ~$50 keep stalling: sellers show up, take profits, cash out. And note: levels are zones, not laser lines. Bounces at 40.00, 39.80 and 40.10 are ONE fat floor around 40.

Resistance · the ceiling

Sellers defend it

Rising price keeps stalling and falling back here. Waiting supply above the market.

Quick check

Floors and ceilings: four questions.

1. Support is best described as…

Support is where buyers keep showing up in force: a safety net of waiting demand under the price.

2. WHY do these levels exist at all?

Thousands of traders remember the level: missed buyers wait there, earlier buyers defend it. Memory makes the map.

3. Price bounced at $40.00, $39.80 and $40.10. How many levels is that?

Levels are zones, not laser lines: a painted stripe, not a tightrope.

4. Each extra time a zone gets tested and holds, traders…

Every successful test adds believers, which is exactly why the eventual BREAK is such a big event. That's next.
