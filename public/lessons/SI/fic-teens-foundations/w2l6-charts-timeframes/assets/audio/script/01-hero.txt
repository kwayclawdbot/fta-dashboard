Last lesson you learned to call a single candle: color, body, wicks, winner. Here's the thing though. No one trades a single candle. Open any trading app and you'll see hundreds of them, marching across the screen. Today, two upgrades. First: the different ways a chart can draw the same prices, and why traders pick candlesticks. Second: the timeframe, the zoom knob that decides how much story each candle holds. By the end, you'll know why the exact same day can look calm on one chart and chaotic on another, and neither one is lying.
