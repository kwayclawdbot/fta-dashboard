Now the zoom knob. Every candle covers a chunk of time, and you choose the chunk. On a daily chart, one candle is one full day. On a one hour chart, that same day explodes into about seven candles. On a fifteen minute chart, twenty six of them. Nothing about the stock changed. Only your zoom did. Zoomed in, every little wiggle gets its own candle, and the day looks wild. Zoomed out, those wiggles fold into one calm bar. That's why traders check more than one timeframe. Zoom out for the big picture, zoom in for the detail, and make sure the two stories agree.
