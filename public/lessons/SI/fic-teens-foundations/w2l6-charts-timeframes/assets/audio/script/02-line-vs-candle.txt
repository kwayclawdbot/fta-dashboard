Start with the simplest chart there is: the line chart. It takes each period's closing price, puts a dot on it, and connects the dots. Clean, easy, great for a quick glance. But look what it throws away. No opens, no highs, no lows, no wicks, no fights. Just the final score of each round, and nothing about how the round went. A candlestick chart keeps all four prices for every period. Same stock, same data, and suddenly you can see every battle: who won, how hard, what got rejected. That's why traders default to candles. The line tells you where price ended. The candles tell you what it took to get there.
