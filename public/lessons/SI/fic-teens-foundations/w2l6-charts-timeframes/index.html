Chart Types

The line tells you where price ended.
Candles tell you what it took.

A line chart connects each period's close — clean, but it throws away the opens, highs, lows and every fight. Candles keep all four prices for every round.

Line chart

The final scores

Dot on each close, connect the dots. Great for a glance — but no bodies, no wicks, no rejections, no battles.

Candlestick chart

The whole fight

Open, high, low, close for every period. Who won, how hard, and which punches got rejected — all visible.

The Zoom Knob

You choose how much time each candle swallows

Daily chart: one candle = one day. One-hour: that day becomes ~7 candles. Fifteen-minute: 26 of them. Nothing about the stock changed — only your zoom did.

Lock It In

Quick check

Four questions on the zoom knob.

1. What does a LINE chart actually plot?

A line chart connects the closes — the final score of each round, nothing about how the round went.

2. Why do traders default to candlestick charts?

Open, high, low, close for every period: who won, how hard, what got rejected.

3. On a 1-hour chart, one candle covers…

The timeframe IS the chunk of time each candle swallows. Daily = a day, 1-hour = an hour, 15-min = fifteen minutes.

4. The same day looks wild on the 15-minute chart and calm on the daily. Who's lying?

Zoomed in, every wiggle gets its own candle; zoomed out they fold into one bar. That's why traders check more than one timeframe.
