The Two Villains

Fear and greed

Two emotions wreck more traders than any bad chart ever will.

Greed

Buy high

Everyone is hyped, so you jump in at the top, scared of missing out.

Fear

Sell low

It dips, everyone panics, so you dump it at the bottom.

Buy high, sell low: the exact opposite of the plan. Feelings are loud, and they are usually wrong at the worst possible moment.

Your Journal Is Your Coach

Write it down, then review it

Every trade gets logged. Over time your journal shows you the truth about your own habits.

Log five things every time: why in, the plan, the exit, the stop, and what happened.

Then read it back. The journal is a mirror. It shows the mistakes you keep repeating and the setups that actually work for you. It is your own coach, and it is free.

Lock It In

Quick check

Five questions. Show me you can keep your cool and trust the plan.

1. Fear and greed usually push traders to:

Greed makes people buy at the top and fear makes them sell at the bottom. That is buy high, sell low, exactly backwards.

2. What is the point of a written trade plan (entry, exit, stop)?

When entry, exit, and stop are decided in advance, fear and greed do not get a vote. Plans beat feelings.

3. Why is cutting a small loss quickly considered a win?

Small losses protect your money for the next opportunity. Big losses are what end accounts.

4. What belongs in a trading journal?

Logging your reasoning and the outcome lets you review, spot repeated mistakes, and actually improve. The journal is your coach.

5. Someone online promises a guaranteed, risk-free 50% return. You should:

No real investment guarantees profits. A promise of high returns with zero risk is a red flag.
