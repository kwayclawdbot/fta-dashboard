Last week you learned what a stock is: a real slice of ownership in a real company. Buy one share of Nike and a tiny piece of that company is yours. Which leaves one very practical question hanging: where do you actually buy it? Who sells it to you? And how does your tap on a screen turn into a trade? That's today's lesson: the stock market itself, the machine that makes buying and selling possible. Every trade you will ever make runs through this machine, so it pays to know how it works. By the end, you'll be able to explain what really happens in the three seconds after someone taps buy. Let's open the doors.
