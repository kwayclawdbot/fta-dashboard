Now watch what happens to an actual order. Say you tap buy on one share, and somewhere out there another investor taps sell on one share of the same company. The exchange's matching engine pairs your buy with their sell at a price you both accept, and the trade is done, usually in under a second. Look at the diagram: order in from the buyer, order in from the seller, matched in the middle. That's every trade, from your ten dollars to a fund's ten million. No handshakes, no trading floor drama. Just buyers and sellers being paired up, millions of times a day.
