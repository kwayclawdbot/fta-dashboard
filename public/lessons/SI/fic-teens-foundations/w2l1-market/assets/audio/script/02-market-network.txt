First, let's clear a myth. The stock market is not one building with a big bell. It's a network of exchanges, like the New York Stock Exchange and the Nasdaq, all connected electronically. Think of it like this. Say you want to sell a pair of limited edition sneakers. You don't stand on a corner hoping a buyer walks past. You list them on a marketplace app, and the app finds the buyer for you. An exchange is that marketplace, just for shares. So the stock market is a giant matching system: the place where everyone who wants to buy meets everyone who wants to sell.
