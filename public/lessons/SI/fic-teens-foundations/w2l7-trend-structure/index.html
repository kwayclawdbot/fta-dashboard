Read the Staircase

Zoom out and price stops looking random: it moves in waves: peaks and valleys. The shape those waves make is the trend, and "the trend is your friend" is the oldest saying in the game.

The Trick

Ignore the candles. Watch peaks & valleys.

Runs to 55, pulls back to 51, climbs to 58, dips to 54. Each peak beats the last (higher highs), each valley is shallower (higher lows). That's an uptrend: a staircase going up.

The Other Two Shapes

Down staircase. Flat hallway.

Every chart on earth is doing one of three things.

Uptrend

Higher highs, higher lows

Buyers in charge. Stairs going up.

Downtrend

Lower highs, lower lows

Sellers in charge. Stairs going down.

Sideways

A range / consolidation

Neither team gains ground: the market catching its breath, often before a big move.

Lock It In

Quick check

Four questions to seal it.

1. The signature of an UPTREND is…

Each peak tops the last, each dip stays shallower: a staircase going up. Red candles happen inside healthy uptrends all the time.

2. A stock prints peaks at $80, $76, $71 and valleys at $74, $69, $65. This is…

Both the peaks and the valleys are stepping down: sellers are in charge.

3. Price keeps bouncing between roughly $40 and $46 for weeks. That's called…

Neither team gains ground: the market catching its breath, often a setup before a bigger move.

4. "The trend is your friend" means…

Go with the flow until the structure clearly changes. And remember from 2.3: it's an odds game, not a promise. No trend lasts forever.
