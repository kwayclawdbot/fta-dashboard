Here's the trick: ignore the individual candles and watch just the peaks and the valleys. Say a stock runs up to fifty five, pulls back to fifty one, climbs to fifty eight, then dips to fifty four. Look at what it's doing. Each peak beats the last peak: fifty five, then fifty eight. Each valley is higher than the last valley: fifty one, then fifty four. Higher highs and higher lows. That's an uptrend, a staircase going up. Buyers are winning each round a little more decisively, and even the setbacks give back less ground each time.
