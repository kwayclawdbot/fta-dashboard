So a trend is structure: higher highs and higher lows going up, lower highs and lower lows going down, and a flat range when neither side wins. But look closer at that sideways hallway. Why did the valleys keep catching at the same floor? Why did the peaks keep stalling at the same ceiling? Those invisible floors and ceilings have names, support and resistance, and they're next. You're about to see the levels every trader on earth is staring at.
