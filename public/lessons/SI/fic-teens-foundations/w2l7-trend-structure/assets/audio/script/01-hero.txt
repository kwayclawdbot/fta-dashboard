Last lesson you zoomed out and saw the full chart: hundreds of candles in a row. And when you zoom out like that, something jumps off the screen. Price doesn't wander randomly. It moves in waves: up moves and pullbacks, peaks and valleys. Today you learn to read the shape those waves make. It's called the trend, and it might be the single most useful read in all of trading. There's a famous saying you'll hear forever: the trend is your friend. By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to look at any chart and classify it in seconds: uptrend, downtrend, or sideways.
