Now run the staircase in reverse. Each peak lower than the last, each valley lower too. Lower highs and lower lows. That's a downtrend, and it means sellers are in charge. And the third shape? Sometimes neither team gains ground. Price just bounces inside a band: peaks stalling around the same level, valleys catching at the same floor. That's sideways, also called a range, or consolidation. It's the market catching its breath, and it's often the setup before a big move. Up staircase, down staircase, flat hallway. Every chart on earth is doing one of the three.
