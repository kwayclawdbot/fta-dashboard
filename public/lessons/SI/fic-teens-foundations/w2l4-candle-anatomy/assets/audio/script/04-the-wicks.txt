Two prices left: the high and the low. During the day, price doesn't just walk in a straight line from open to close. It wanders. The high is the most expensive it got all day, the low is the cheapest. Say our stock spiked up to fifty-five at one point and dipped to forty-nine at another. Those two extremes get drawn as thin lines poking out of the body, one above, one below. Look at the thin lines on the diagram. Those are the wicks. The upper wick reaches up to the high, fifty-five. The lower wick reaches down to the low, forty-nine. Body is open-to-close. Wicks are the full range price traveled.
