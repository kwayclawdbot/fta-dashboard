Let's build one candle from scratch, all four prices together. Our stock opened at fifty, so the body starts at fifty. It closed at fifty-four, so the body ends at fifty-four. It touched a high of fifty-five, so a short wick pokes up to fifty-five. It touched a low of forty-nine, so a wick drops down to forty-nine. That's a complete candle. Four numbers, one shape. Open, close, high, low. The block in the middle is the body, the two threads are the wicks. This exact shape is what a chart is made of, over and over.
