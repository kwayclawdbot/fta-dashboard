Now the second price: the close. The close is the price when the day ended. Say our stock finishes the day at fifty-four. Mark fifty-four. Here's the useful part. Take that stretch from the open at fifty up to the close at fifty-four and fill it in as a solid block. Watch it fill on screen now. That solid block has a name: the body. So the body is always the distance from the open to the close, nothing more. Two prices in, and you've already got the fat part of the candle.
