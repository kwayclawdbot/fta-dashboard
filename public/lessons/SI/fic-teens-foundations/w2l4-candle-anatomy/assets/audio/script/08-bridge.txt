So, a candlestick is four prices in one shape: open, close, high, low, drawn as a body plus two wicks. That's the object. But notice what we did NOT do today. We never said whether the buyers or the sellers actually won this candle. A candle's parts are one thing. Its color and what that color is screaming at you is another. That's exactly where we go next lesson: reading a candle to call the winner, green team or red team, in a single glance. You've got the alphabet. Next, we start reading.
