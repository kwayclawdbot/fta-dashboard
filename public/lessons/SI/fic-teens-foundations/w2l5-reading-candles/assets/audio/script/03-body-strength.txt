Color says who won. The body says how hard they won. A long body means the open and close are far apart: one side steamrolled the other, start to finish. A short body means the close barely moved from the open: a close fight, almost a draw. And when the body shrinks to nearly nothing, so the open and close are basically the same price, that candle has a name: a doji. A doji is a stalemate, a rope frozen mid tug of war, and it often warns that the current move is running out of muscle.
