Last lesson you took a candlestick apart: open, close, high, low, drawn as a body and two wicks. You can label the parts of any candle in seconds. But a labelled candle is like a scoreboard with no score. Today you learn the score: how to glance at one candle and call who won that round, the buyers or the sellers, and by how much. This is the skill you'll use on every chart for the rest of this course, thousands of times, in under a second each. Let's learn to call the fight.
