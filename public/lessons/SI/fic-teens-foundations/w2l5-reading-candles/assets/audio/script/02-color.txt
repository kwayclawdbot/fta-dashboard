Start with the fastest signal on the chart: color. Say a stock opens at fifty and closes at fifty four. It ended higher than it started, so the candle prints green. Buyers won that round. Now say it opens at fifty and closes at forty seven. It ended lower, so the candle prints red. Sellers won. That's the whole rule. Green means the close beat the open. Red means it didn't. One glance, and you know which team took the round.
