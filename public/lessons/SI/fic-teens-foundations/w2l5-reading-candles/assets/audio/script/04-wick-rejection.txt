Now the sneaky one: the wicks. A long wick shows a push that failed. Picture a candle with a long lower wick. At some point, sellers shoved the price way down there, but buyers slapped it right back up before the close. That's called a rejection. The market tried a price and said: no thanks, not today. A long wick pointing down often means buyers are defending. A long wick pointing up means sellers smacked a rally back down. The wick shows the punch. The close shows who was standing at the bell.
