Lock It In

Quick check

Five questions. Nail them to earn your points for the lesson.

1. Which is the best description of compounding?

Compounding is growth on top of growth. Your gains start making their own gains, so it snowballs the longer you leave it alone.

2. Your friend keeps their birthday money in a savings account; you buy a share of a company you like. What's the key difference?

Saving keeps money safe but flat. Investing gives it a job so it can grow. Over years, that difference gets huge thanks to compounding.

3. Maya starts investing a small amount at 15. Jordan waits and invests a bigger amount starting at 40. Who has the biggest advantage working for them?

Starting early beats starting big. Maya's money has an extra 25 years to compound, an edge Jordan can't buy back later.

4. On the time-machine slider, the growth multiple starts slow, then takes off. What single thing is doing that heavy lifting?

Time is the lever. The longer money compounds, the more the growth stacks on itself, which is why the curve rips upward at the end.

5. The 8% growth rate on the slider is best described as:

The rate is illustrative. It shows the shape of compounding. Real markets rise and fall, and no one can guarantee a return.
