First, the break itself. Say our stock has bonked its head on the fifty dollar ceiling four times. Sellers defended it every time. Then one day, buyers come in heavy, and price punches through fifty and keeps going. Fifty one. Fifty two. That's a breakout: price escaping a level that used to hold it. A breakout is the market announcing that the old balance is dead. The sellers who owned that ceiling? Beaten. And the same thing happens in reverse when a floor gives way: price falls through support, and the slide is on.
