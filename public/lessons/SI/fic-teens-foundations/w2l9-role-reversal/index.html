Lock It In

Quick check

Breaks and flips — bring it home.

1. A breakout is…

Price escapes its cage — through resistance upward, or through support downward. The old balance is dead.

2. Price breaks ABOVE $50 resistance, then drifts back down to $50. Traders now expect…

Role reversal: missed buyers wait at $50 to buy the retest, and breakout buyers defend their entry.

3. When a support FLOOR breaks, that old level often becomes…

The flip works both directions — a broken floor turns into the new ceiling. (And remember 2.3: it's odds, never guarantees.)

4. WHY does a broken level flip teams?

Same force that built the level in 2.8: memory. Missed buyers wait there; breakout buyers defend it.
