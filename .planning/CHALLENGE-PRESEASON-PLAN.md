# The 5-Day Challenge: Pre-Season Plan
**Free users in the app, active for 30 days, before Day 1 ever starts.**
*Drafted 2026-07-27 · companion to CHALLENGE-FUNNEL-REVIEW.md and the C7 cohort machine*

## The plan in one paragraph
The 5-Day Challenge (Sept 1–5, free, no card at signup) is the conversion event. But a registrant who signs up August 3rd and hears nothing until September 1st is a cold email address, and 30–40% of those never show. So the month of August is run as a **pre-season**: every challenge registrant gets a free app account the moment they sign up, gets a win inside 30 minutes, and is kept genuinely active — practicing, learning, earning XP — for the weeks before kickoff. By Day 1 they aren't prospects showing up to a webinar. They're users defending a streak.

## Why free-first works for us
- **No card, no friction, real habit.** The challenge promise is "no card at signup." Pre-season keeps that promise while building the one thing that predicts both completion and purchase: an existing usage habit.
- **The app sells itself before we ever pitch.** The $1,500 offer (FTA Lifetime + 1 year of Club) lands on Day 3 of the challenge. A registrant who has spent three weeks inside the HQ — simulator, lessons, community — is evaluating an upgrade to something they already use, not a cold purchase.
- **Progress carries over.** XP, belts, watchlists, and streaks earned in pre-season carry into the challenge. Nobody starts Sept 1 from zero, so nobody wants to skip Sept 1.

## The registrant journey
**Minute 0 — signup.** Challenge registration creates the free account on the spot (funnel steps 5–6). Confirmation page: add-to-calendar, SMS opt-in, community intro post, "what happens next" timeline.

**Day 0 — the first win (≤30 minutes).** One mission: name your first watchlist stock and write one sentence on why, post it in Community. A completed artifact on day zero is the strongest activation signal we have.

**Weeks 1–4 of August — the rhythm.** The free tier runs a light weekly loop, never more than ~15 min/day:
- **Today's One Thing** — the HQ always shows exactly one next step.
- **Simulator** — $100k paper account; weekly "practice rep" prompt.
- **Free lessons** — the TF fundamentals track, one short lesson per week.
- **Wednesday live free class** — the existing weekly session doubles as pre-season campfire.
- **Community beat** — one prompt a week (share your watchlist, call a chart's trend, post a question). Lurkers don't learn; posters show up Sept 1.
- **XP + streaks** — visible progress bar toward "Challenge Ready" status; registrants who complete the four weekly beats earn a pre-season badge that shows on the Sept 1 leaderboard from minute one.

**Final week (Aug 25–31) — cohort forming.** Countdown flips on in-app and in email. "Your cohort is forming" splash, cohort roster fills in, day-before SMS + morning-of SMS (the show-up doublers), calendar re-confirm.

**Sept 1–5 — the challenge itself.** Five daily missions, live sessions, leaderboard. Day 3: the soft pitch. Sept 6: access deadline closes the offer window.

## What we measure (C7 dashboard)
1. **Activation rate** — % of registrants completing the Day-0 mission within 48h (the number that matters most).
2. **Weekly active** — % touching the app each pre-season week; watch the week-2 sag.
3. **Show-up rate** — % of registrants active on Sept 1 (benchmark: sequence + SMS should roughly double a cold baseline).
4. **Finisher rate** — 5/5-day completions; purchases concentrate here (15–30% of finishers vs 5–12% of all registrants).

## Dependencies (already flagged elsewhere)
- **Twilio token** — the SMS show-up machine is the single biggest lever and is dead until replaced. (Owner)
- **P1 funnel fixes** from CHALLENGE-FUNNEL-REVIEW: challenge-variant date copy, brand coherence, social-proof threshold, `src` attribution, audience-rule copy. (Hours of work, before August marketing spend)
- **Free-tier surface check** — confirm exactly which lessons/simulator/community surfaces the free tier exposes, so pre-season promises match gates.

## The one rule
Pre-season never pitches. August is free value, rhythm, and identity ("I'm in the September cohort"). The first ask for money happens Day 3 of the challenge, exactly once armed, exactly as the email machine already does it.
